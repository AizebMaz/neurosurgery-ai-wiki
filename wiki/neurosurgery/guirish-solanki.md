# Guirish A. Solanki

Neurosurgeon who developed [[posterior-calvarial-augmentation|Posterior Calvarial Augmentation (PCA)]] using distraction osteogenesis for treating craniosynostosis with posterior calvarial deficiency.

## Contributions

### Development of PCA
- Pioneered posterior calvarial vault expansion using distraction osteogenesis in **2006**
- Published initial series with early follow-up results in **2009**
- Collaborated with Mr Steve Dover and Mr Hiroshi Nishikawa

### Key Innovations
- Applied [[ilizarov|Ilizarov's]] distraction principles to cranial vault
- Developed technique for gradual posterior expansion
- Demonstrated regression of hindbrain hernia without foramen magnum decompression
- Showed deflation of syringomyelia through posterior fossa expansion

### Technique Evolution
- Initially used 4 distractors, refined to 2
- Evolved from 3-screw to 6-9 screw baseplates
- Reduced transcutaneous extension
- Standardized rate: 0.5 mm twice daily

## Clinical Philosophy

Focus on addressing root cause of posterior cranial pathology:
- Rather than decompressing foramen magnum for Chiari, expand posterior fossa
- Gradual distraction preferred over immediate expansion in young children
- Multidisciplinary team approach essential
