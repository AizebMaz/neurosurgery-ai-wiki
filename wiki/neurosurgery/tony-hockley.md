# Tony Hockley

British craniofacial surgeon whose philosophy guided the development of [[posterior-calvarial-augmentation|Posterior Calvarial Augmentation]].

## Guiding Philosophy

> "If the problem is at the back of the head, operate at the back of the head."
> — Tony Hockley, 2008

This principle challenged the prevailing approach of performing [[foramen-magnum-decompression|foramen magnum decompression]] for [[chiari-malformation|Chiari malformation]] in craniosynostosis, instead advocating for addressing the posterior calvarial deficiency directly.

## Historical Context

In 1996, Hockley and colleagues at Birmingham Children's Hospital first reported experience with posterior calvarial release for craniosynostosis, laying groundwork for later PCA development.

## Influence

His philosophy directly influenced [[guirish-solanki|Guirish Solanki's]] work:
- Shifted focus from decompressing foramen magnum to expanding posterior fossa
- Led to development of PCA as primary treatment
- Established posterior approach as standard for posterior pathology
