# Foramen Magnum Decompression

Traditional surgical treatment for [[chiari-malformation|Chiari malformation]] involving removal of bone at the foramen magnum to create more space for the hindbrain. While effective, [[posterior-calvarial-augmentation|Posterior Calvarial Augmentation (PCA)]] offers an alternative that addresses the root cause.

## Traditional Technique

1. Suboccipital craniectomy
2. Removal of posterior arch of C1
3. Dural opening
4. Duraplasty (enlarging dural sac)

## Limitations

- Requires dural opening
- Risk of CSF leak
- Risk of infection
- Doesn't address underlying small posterior fossa
- May require revision

## Comparison to PCA

| Aspect | Foramen Magnum Decompression | Posterior Calvarial Augmentation |
|--------|------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Approach | Decompresses outlet | Expands posterior fossa |
| Dural opening | Required | Not required |
| CSF leak risk | Higher | Lower |
| Root cause | Symptom treatment | Volume correction |
| Invasiveness | More | Less |

## Current Role

- Still indicated in some cases
- Contraindication for PCA: posterior VP shunt
- May be needed if PCA insufficient
