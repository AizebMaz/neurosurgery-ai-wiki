# AI in Surgical Planning for Craniosynostosis

Integration of artificial intelligence with craniofacial surgery planning, particularly for procedures like [[posterior-calvarial-augmentation|Posterior Calvarial Augmentation (PCA)]].

## Potential Applications

### Preoperative Planning
- **3D reconstruction analysis**: Automated segmentation of CT/MRI
- **Volume calculations**: Predicting expansion needed
- **Vector optimization**: AI-suggested distractor placement
- **Risk prediction**: Identifying venous sinus variants

### Intraoperative Guidance
- **Real-time navigation**: Computer vision for screw placement
- **Dural protection**: Alert systems for thin bone
- **Symmetry assessment**: Live feedback on expansion

### Postoperative Monitoring
- **Distraction tracking**: Automated measurement of expansion
- **Complication prediction**: Early warning systems
- **Outcome prediction**: Expected vs. achieved results

## Current State

Most applications remain experimental or developmental:
- 3D reconstruction widely used but manually supervised
- AI-powered surgical planning not yet standard
- Potential for reducing operative time and complications

## Integration with PCA

Specific opportunities:
- Predicting optimal distraction rate per patient
- Modeling individual biomechanics
- Personalizing consolidation timeline
- Automated distraction monitoring
