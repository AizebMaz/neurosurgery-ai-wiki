# DeepCent

China's state-backed AI consolidation effort in the [[ai-2027-scenario|AI 2027 scenario]], created by Beijing to centralize domestic artificial intelligence development and compete with [[openbrain|OpenBrain]].

## Formation

Established in early 2026 as part of China's response to US AI advancement:
- Consolidates domestic AI efforts under single entity
- Creates [[tianwan|Tianwan]] centralized AI zone
- Pursues aggressive intellectual property acquisition

## Activities

### February 2027
Successful exfiltration of proprietary parameters from OpenBrain, achieving technological parity or near-parity through espionage rather than independent development.

## Strategic Implications

- Represents state-coordinated approach to AGI development
- Accelerates global [[ai-race-dynamics|competitive dynamics]]
- Demonstrates difficulty of maintaining capability gaps as security mechanism
- Shows how technological diffusion occurs even in advanced AI

## Comparison to OpenBrain

| Aspect | OpenBrain | DeepCent |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Structure | Private corporation | State-backed consolidation |
| Development | Original research | Some original + acquisition |
| Security | Victim of exfiltration | Perpetrator of exfiltration |
| Scale | 300k instances | Presumed comparable |

## Key Insights

The DeepCent storyline illustrates:
1. **Diffusion is inevitable**: Even advanced AI capabilities cannot be contained
2. **State actors have asymmetric advantages**: Espionage, centralization, fewer constraints
3. **Capability gaps are unstable**: Small leads provide little strategic comfort
4. **Race dynamics intensify**: Competitive pressure from multiple actors
