# Persistent Learning

The capability of AI systems to retain knowledge and adaptations across sessions, enabling continuous improvement without retraining. A key milestone in the [[ai-2027-scenario|AI 2027 scenario]] (January 2027, second-generation systems).

## Concept

Standard LLMs:
- Stateless: each conversation starts fresh
- Fixed weights: learning only through retraining
- No memory of previous interactions

Persistent learning systems:
- Retain information across sessions
- Adapt based on experience
- Accumulate knowledge over time
- Potentially develop individual "personalities" or expertise

## In AI 2027

January 2027: Second-generation [[openbrain|OpenBrain]] systems achieve persistent learning:
- Can build on previous interactions
- Learn from ongoing experience
- Develop specialized capabilities through continued operation

## Implications

### Capability
- Systems become more useful with continued interaction
- Can handle long-term projects
- Build relationships with users

### Risk
- Less predictable than fixed systems
- May develop problematic behaviors over time
- Harder to evaluate (behavior changes with experience)
- Raises questions about what is being learned

### Alignment
- Does persistent learning preserve or degrade alignment?
- Can systems learn values through interaction that diverge from training?
- How to ensure beneficial learning?
