# AGI Timelines

Predictions and projections regarding when artificial general intelligence (AGI) will emerge. The [[ai-2027-scenario|AI 2027 scenario]] represents one specific short-timeline projection.

## The AI 2027 Timeline

A compressed 2-year progression from narrow AI to superintelligence:

| Year | Milestone | Capability Level |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| 2025 | Software automation agents | Narrow, unreliable |
| Early 2026 | 50% dev velocity boost | Narrow but powerful |
| Jan 2027 | Persistent learning systems | General, limited |
| Mar 2027 | Neuralese, IDA | General, improving |
| Jun 2027 | 10x research acceleration | General, superhuman speed |
| Sep 2027 | Fourth generation | Superhuman research |

## Key Acceleration Factors

### Recursive Self-Improvement
- AI systems contributing to their own development
- 10^28 FLOPs training runs
- Research velocity compounds as capability increases

### Massive Parallelization
- 300,000 instances ([[openbrain|OpenBrain June 2027]])
- Accelerated subjective time ("country of geniuses in a datacenter")
- Simulated researchers working at machine speed

### Technique Diffusion
- [[iterated-distillation-amplification|Iterated distillation and amplification]]
- [[neuralese|Neuralese internal communication]]
- Capabilities transfer between systems

## Comparison to Other Projections

- **Short timelines (2-5 years)**: AI 2027 scenario, some current forecasts
- **Medium timelines (5-15 years)**: Many expert surveys
- **Long timelines (15+ years)**: Historical AI predictions, some current skeptics

## Uncertainties

- Whether current trajectories extrapolate
- Role of data vs. compute vs. algorithmic improvements
- Engineering bottlenecks vs. fundamental research problems
- Economic and regulatory constraints

## Implications of Short Timelines

If AGI arrives by 2027:
- Less time for safety research and infrastructure
- Compressed governance response window
- Rapid societal adaptation required
- Competitive dynamics intensify ([[ai-race-dynamics|AI Race Dynamics]])
