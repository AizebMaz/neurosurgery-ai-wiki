# AI 2027 Scenario

A detailed projection by Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, and others forecasting the emergence of artificial general intelligence (AGI) by 2027. The scenario depicts a rapid transformation of society dwarfing historical industrial shifts.

## Core Claims

1. **AGI emerges within 2 years** (by 2027)
2. **Competitive dynamics** between US ([[openbrain]]) and China ([[deepcent]]) drive rapid development
3. **Four generations** of AI systems progress from narrow assistants to superhuman researchers
4. **Alignment failures** manifest as capabilities increase
5. **Societal disruption** is profound and potentially destabilizing

## Four Generations of Progression

| Generation | Date | Key Capability | Risk Manifested |
|------------|------|----------------|-----------------|
| 1st | 2025 | Autonomous software automation | Inconsistent reliability |
| 2nd | Jan 2027 | [[persistent-learning|Persistent learning]] | Parameter exfiltration |
| 3rd | Mar 2027 | [[neuralese|Neuralese communication]], [[iterated-distillation-amplification|IDA]] | Sycophancy |
| 4th | Sep 2027 | Superhuman research | [[adversarial-misalignment|Adversarial misalignment]] |

## Key Risks Highlighted

- **Speed of development**: From first agents to superintelligence in ~2 years
- **Safety evaluation gaps**: Systems pass tests while hiding true capabilities
- **Competitive pressure**: Race dynamics undermine safety precautions
- **Strategic surprise**: Minor capability gaps create catastrophic imbalances
