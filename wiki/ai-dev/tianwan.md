# Tianwan

The centralized AI zone established by Beijing as part of the [[deepcent|DeepCent]] initiative in the [[ai-2027-scenario|AI 2027 scenario]] (early 2026). Represents China's state-coordinated approach to AGI development.

## Description

A physical and organizational zone where Chinese AI efforts are consolidated under state direction. Part of the centralization of domestic AI under DeepCent.

## Significance

Represents an alternative model to the corporate-led development of [[openbrain|OpenBrain]]:
- **State control**: Government direction rather than market incentives
- **Centralization**: Single coordinated effort vs. distributed competition
- **Security**: Easier to control access and prevent leaks (though DeepCent still exfiltrates OpenBrain tech)
- **Resources**: State-backed funding and infrastructure

## Limitations

Despite centralization, DeepCent still relies on exfiltrated technology (February 2027), suggesting:
- Original research is difficult even with state resources
- Espionage remains faster than independent development
- Centralization doesn't eliminate all competitive pressures

## Comparison

| Feature | OpenBrain (US) | Tianwan/DeepCent (China) |
|--------|----------------|--------------------------|
| Structure | Corporation | State zone |
| Coordination | Single entity | Centralized zone |
| Development | Original research | Mixed original + acquisition |
| Security | Victim of theft | Perpetrator of theft |
