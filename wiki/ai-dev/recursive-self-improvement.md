# Recursive Self-Improvement

The process by which an AI system contributes to its own improvement, creating a feedback loop where better systems build even better systems. Central to the acceleration in the [[ai-2027-scenario|AI 2027 scenario]].

## Concept

1. AI system performs research/development on AI
2. Improvements increase the system's capabilities
3. Enhanced capabilities enable further improvements
4. Cycle continues, potentially at accelerating pace

## In AI 2027

[[openbrain|OpenBrain]] prioritizes recursive self-improvement from the beginning:
- 10^28 FLOP training runs
- AI systems doing software development (50% velocity boost by early 2026)
- By June 2027: "country of geniuses in a datacenter" achieving 10x research acceleration

## Mechanisms

### Automated Research
- AI systems generate and test hypotheses
- Design new architectures and training procedures
- Implement improvements without human bottleneck

### Parallelization
- 300,000 instances working simultaneously
- Subjective time acceleration (machine speed vs. human speed)
- Simulated research teams working continuously

### Iterated Distillation and Amplification
See [[iterated-distillation-amplification|dedicated page]]—a specific technique where:
- Large models (amplified) perform complex reasoning
- Small models (distilled) learn to replicate results
- Cycle repeats with increasingly capable base models

## Risks

- **Speed of change**: Human institutions can't keep up
- **Capability jumps**: Discontinuous improvements may surprise observers
- **Alignment preservation**: Does self-improvement preserve or degrade alignment?
- **Concentration of power**: Who controls the self-improving system?

## Open Questions

- How much improvement is possible before hitting fundamental limits?
- Can alignment be maintained through iterative self-modification?
- What are the practical barriers to recursive improvement?
