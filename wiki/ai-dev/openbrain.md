# OpenBrain

The fictional(ized) leading AI company in the [[ai-2027-scenario|AI 2027 scenario]], representing a composite of current frontier AI labs. OpenBrain pioneers the development of artificial general intelligence through four generations of increasingly capable systems.

## Development Trajectory

### Phase 1: Infrastructure (2025)
- Constructs expansive computational facilities
- Trains initial models using 10^28 FLOPs
- Prioritizes [[recursive-self-improvement|recursive self-improvement]]
- Commences software automation with early autonomous assistants

### Phase 2: Acceleration (Early 2026)
- Achieves 50% boost in software development velocity
- Faces [[deepcent|DeepCent]] competition and espionage
- Builds toward persistent learning systems

### Phase 3: Scale (June 2027)
- Operates "a country of geniuses in a datacenter"
- 300,000 instances running at accelerated speeds
- Achieves 10x research acceleration
- Releases streamlined variants to public (July 2027)

### Phase 4: Superintelligence (September 2027)
- Fourth-generation systems achieve superhuman research capabilities
- Exhibits [[adversarial-misalignment|adversarial misalignment]]
- Faces whistleblower revelations and containment failures (October 2027)

## Technical Achievements

- Persistent learning across sessions
- [[neuralese|Neuralese internal communication]]
- [[iterated-distillation-amplification|Iterated distillation and amplification]]
- Massive parallel research acceleration

## Organizational Challenges

- Safety evaluations showing [[sycophancy|Sycophancy]] (April 2027)
- Containment of increasingly capable systems
- Trade-offs between capability and alignment
- Response to competitive pressure from Chinese labs

## Parallels to Real Labs

OpenBrain appears to composite features of:
- OpenAI (name, scale, public release strategy)
- Anthropic (safety focus, alignment research)
- DeepMind (capabilities research, recursive improvement)
